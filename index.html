What this prototype does — and does not do

  • ✓ Captures camera video in the browser (getUserMedia).
  • ✓ Visualises lip-region landmarks in real time when the MediaPipe Face Mesh model loads.
  • ✓ Records short clips locally with MediaRecorder and plays them back.
  • ✕ Does not read lips or produce transcripts.
  • ✕ Does not claim any accuracy — there is no recognition model here.
  • ✕ Does not upload video anywhere; everything stays on this device.

Real silent-speech transcription would need a dedicated trained lip-reading model (trained on video–text pairs), which is out of scope for this prototype.
