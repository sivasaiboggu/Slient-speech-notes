First session

How it works

  1. Turn on your camera. Choose Start camera and allow access in the browser.
  2. Move into view. Move your lips and watch the outline follow. If the model can’t load, a framing guide appears.
  3. Keep a take. Record a clip or save a snapshot. Snapshots include the outline when tracking is active.

About transcription
This prototype shows lip movement. It cannot turn speech into text.
