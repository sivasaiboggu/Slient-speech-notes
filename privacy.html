Silent-Speech Notes

Camera and privacy

The camera turns on when you choose Start camera and grant access. The app requests video only. Choose Stop camera to release it any time. You can also revoke permission in your browser settings.

What stays in your browser

Live video and lip landmarks are processed in the current tab. The latest 12 unsaved clips stay in this tab until you refresh or close it. When you save a note, its text and clip are stored together in this browser. Saved notes appear when you reopen the workspace on the same browser and profile. Delete a saved note to remove its local copy, or clear this site’s data to remove all saved notes. Downloads are separate files on your device.

What the app loads

After camera access is granted, the browser downloads the tracking runtime from jsDelivr and model weights from Google Cloud Storage. Both downloads use ordinary web requests. Your video, landmarks, clips and notes stay on your device. Your theme choice is saved in your browser’s site storage.
