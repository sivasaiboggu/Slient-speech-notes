Silent-Speech Notes

Camera and privacy

The camera turns on when you choose Start camera and grant access. The app requests video only. Choose Stop camera to release it any time. You can also revoke permission in your browser settings.

What stays in your browser

Live video and lip landmarks are processed in the current tab. Clips stay in memory and are cleared when you refresh or close the tab. Delete a clip to remove it from the session. Downloaded clips and snapshots stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

What the app loads

After camera access is granted, the browser downloads the tracking runtime from jsDelivr and model weights from Google Cloud Storage. Both downloads use ordinary web requests. Your video, landmarks and clips stay on your device. Your theme choice is saved in your browser’s site storage.
